Read this first

  • This is a rough early version. A beta may lose your ride data, so export rides you care about.
  • iPhone: do not lock the phone; just put it in your pocket. The screen goes black by itself and the app keeps running. Pressing the side button stops the ride; what was recorded before that is kept. Pockets that cover the top of the phone work best. An arm strap leaves the screen on.
  • Android: lock it or not, either works.
  • Rating by tapping your pocket is experimental. How well it works while skating has not been measured yet, and it needs a hand free to tap.
  • There is no shared map, and the app is not in the Play Store or App Store.
  • Do not use your phone while skating if it is not safe to. We have no evidence about safety.

Install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source (your browser or file manager). Allow it for this install; you can switch it off again afterwards in Settings.
  3. Tap Install, then open SkateButter.

Update

Download the newer APK and install it over the old one. Your rides stay on the phone. Do not uninstall first, as that deletes them. Updates only work from the same signed source, so install every version from this page.

What to test

  • Does a ride record from start to stop, and does it show up afterwards?
  • Does the route colouring match how the surface felt?
  • Pocket taps: do two taps (good) and three taps (bad) register, and how often do they not? Note the surface and your speed.
  • Anything that crashes, freezes or loses a ride.
