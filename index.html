SkateButter remembers how smooth your skate routes were, and lets you rate the surface with a tap on your pocket.

Early test version. SkateButter is not in any app store and is still rough. You can read about the test builds on the beta page.

What it does

  • See how your ride felt. The app records your route and colours it by surface, using the phone's motion sensors. The colouring is an early estimate.
  • Rate without stopping (experimental). Two taps for good, three for bad, answered by sound and vibration, with the phone in your pocket. We are still testing how well this works while skating.
  • Keep your routes. Name them and come back to them. Both test versions can save a named route and reopen it on the map.
  • Your rides live on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, and no SkateButter server. Some data can still leave your phone, though: the privacy page lists what and when.
